# Quickstart: 醍醐的海岸骑行

## Run locally

From `easy-case/`, run:

```powershell
python -m http.server 4173
```

Open `http://localhost:4173`.

## Browser acceptance walkthrough

1. At desktop size, confirm 醍醐, bicycle, sunny coast, a continuous sand shoreline and a coast road that follows it as a separate surface.
2. Observe at least three five-shot cycles; confirm framing changes, bicycle pedaling, sea/shore/road boundaries, parallax and loop transition remain coherent.
3. Trigger the manual water break; verify the bicycle stops with its kickstand down, 醍醐 leaves the bike, lowers the bill into the freshwater stream, lifts it to swallow, then returns to the bike. Confirm no isolated head dip substitutes for the full action.
4. Pause/resume and trigger a manual water break with pointer and keyboard; confirm the prior play state is restored.
5. Use Tab, Enter and Space; inspect visible focus and live status.
6. At 360 px width, verify no horizontal page overflow and reachable primary controls.
7. Enable `prefers-reduced-motion`; verify no continuous motion and all essential content remains visible.
8. Check browser console and Network panel for errors or remote asset requests.

Observed results are recorded in [verification.md](verification.md).
