# Quickstart: 醍醐的海岸骑行

## Run locally

From `easy-case/`, run:

```powershell
python -m http.server 4173
```

Open `http://localhost:4173`.

## Browser acceptance walkthrough

1. At desktop size, confirm 醍醐, bicycle, sunny coast and road are immediately identifiable; check the low three-quarter perspective and depth layers.
2. Observe at least three five-shot cycles; confirm framing changes, bicycle pedaling, scenery parallax and loop transition remain coherent.
3. Pause/resume and trigger a manual water break with pointer and keyboard; confirm the prior play state is restored.
4. Use Tab, Enter and Space; inspect visible focus and live status.
5. At 360 px width, verify no horizontal page overflow and reachable primary controls.
6. Enable `prefers-reduced-motion`; verify no continuous motion and all essential content remains visible.
7. Check browser console and Network panel for errors or remote asset requests.

Observed results are recorded in [verification.md](verification.md).
